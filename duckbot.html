Duckbot is a planned project and we don't yet know, when we'll start working on it. But according to the plan, it will be a small AI voice assistant with a possibility to drive itself around. It will be a lot of work, because we'll need to figure out how to use ESP-32's connection to the internet and than to develop our own app for Android. We'll need to fit ton of componetnts into a small body and get them all powered by single battery. Because of it being such complicated project, we might make DUCK V2 first.
